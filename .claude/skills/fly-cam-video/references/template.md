# Frozen-Time / Slow-Motion Camera Prompt — Reusable Template

The same structure behind all worked examples, generalized with [blanks] — drop in
any red-line-marked photo, fill the brackets, done. Target: Seedance 2.5
image-to-video (InVideo Agent Two, Higgsfield, or any Seedance interface).

```
Image-to-video, [DURATION] seconds, ONE continuous flying camera shot, no cuts.

FIRST FRAME: the video starts as an EXACT copy of the attached image — [DESCRIBE
THE SCENE: era / location / subject / mood], including the red drawn line and its
arrow-marked ends. Within the first half second the red markings dissolve
completely and must NEVER reappear.

CONCEPT — [FROZEN TIME / SLOW-MOTION SUSPENSION]: as the markings vanish, the
photograph gains full three-dimensional depth, but [TIME STAYS COMPLETELY FROZEN /
TIME DROPS INTO EXTREME SLOW MOTION]. [DESCRIBE WHAT STAYS PERFECTLY STILL, AND
WHAT CARRIES ONE TINY MICRO-MOVEMENT — e.g. dust hanging in the air, a chest still
rising and falling, fabric rippling]. NOTHING moves at normal speed except the
camera, which drifts through the moment slowly and deliberately, inspecting
details and faces at close range.

KEEP THE ORIGINAL [BLACK-AND-WHITE / COLOR] LOOK: [DESCRIBE THE PHOTOGRAPHIC STYLE
— grain, light, tone, weather]. All signage and detail stays EXACTLY as in the
photo and perfectly legible: [LIST THE KEY TEXT / OBJECTS / WARDROBE TO PRESERVE
EXACTLY]. Strictly [ERA / LOCATION]-accurate throughout.

CAMERA PATH: the red line is the flight trajectory — enter at the arrow near
[MARKER 1 — WHERE THE SHOT BEGINS] (marker 1), end at the arrow [MARKER 2 — WHERE
THE SHOT ENDS] (marker 2).

00–0Xs: Static frame identical to the input; red markings dissolve. [FIRST HINT
THAT TIME IS FROZEN / SLOWED — e.g. dust motes hanging, a wisp of haze drifting].

[TIMESTAMP RANGE]: The camera [MOVES HOW — dives / rises / sweeps / glides] to
[SUBJECT OR DETAIL 1] — [WHAT THE CLOSE-UP REVEALS].

[TIMESTAMP RANGE]: [Repeat this beat for SUBJECT 2, SUBJECT 3, SUBJECT 4 … — one
beat per stop along the drawn red-line path, moving from marker 1 toward marker 2,
each with its own close-up detail.]

[FINAL TIMESTAMP RANGE]: THE PAYOFF — time suddenly UNFREEZES / snaps to real time:
[DESCRIBE THE SNAP-BACK MOTION FOR EACH SUBJECT], and the frame cuts to black
mid-motion.

Physics & consistency: [FREEZE / SLOW-MOTION RULE] until the payoff — not a single
object or person moves out of turn; rock-solid geometry on every face and
letterform from every angle; no morphing, no melting features; the camera never
clips through objects.

AUDIO: a frozen/slowed world — near silence with a low, dreamlike drone; soft airy
whooshes as the camera passes objects; faint reverberant echoes of a [LOCATION
TYPE] suspended in time. At the payoff, sound SNAPS on with the unfreeze: [LIST
THE SOUNDS THAT RETURN], full ambience for the final two seconds.
```

## Fill-in guidance

- **DURATION**: 30 seconds is the house standard. Budget ~1s opening static beat,
  5–7 subject beats of 3–5s each, and ~2s payoff.
- **MARKER 1 / MARKER 2**: describe them by what is physically there in the photo
  ("the arrow near the van's grille", "the arrow exiting frame toward the parked
  cars, top-left"), never by abstract coordinates.
- If the photo has **no red markings**, delete the dissolve sentences and instead
  state the path fully in words ("no red lines or drawn markings anywhere").
- For a **spiral/loop path**, replace CAMERA PATH with: "ONE UNBROKEN LOOP: the
  camera traces a single rising-and-falling spiral, never reversing hard or
  snapping — always mid-arc, already banking into the next curve before the last
  one finishes," then keep the timestamped beats riding that arc.
