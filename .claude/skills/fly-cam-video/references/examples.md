# Fly Cam — Three Complete Worked Examples (Seedance 2.5)

Each prompt was written for a specific red-line-marked source photo. Use them as
style/density references: match your scene to the closest one and mirror its voice.

- **Example 1 — Old Calcutta Street**: B&W archival photo · hard frozen time ·
  S-curve line path with markers · 30s.
- **Example 2 — Protest Barricade**: present-day color photojournalism · slow-motion
  suspension (no hard freeze) · one unbroken spiral loop, clean plate (no markings) · 30s.
- **Example 3 — Benares Ghats, Varanasi**: present-day color · frozen time with one
  subject at a time in micro-motion · continuous glide with markers · 30s.

---

## Example 1 — Old Calcutta Street (Frozen Time, B&W)

Source: 1960s black-and-white photograph of a crowded Calcutta tram street; red
line drawn from the van's grille (marker 1) to the top-left frame exit (marker 2).

```
Image-to-video, 30 seconds, ONE continuous flying camera shot, no cuts.

FIRST FRAME: the video starts as an EXACT copy of the attached image — a 1960s
black-and-white photograph of a crowded Calcutta street (tram car "121," ESPLANADE
route), including the red drawn line and its two arrow-marked ends. Within the
first half second the red markings dissolve completely and must NEVER reappear.

CONCEPT — FROZEN TIME: as the markings vanish, the photograph gains full three-
dimensional depth, but TIME STAYS COMPLETELY FROZEN. Every person, vehicle,
particle of dust and swinging load is locked mid-motion like a vast sculpture: a
running boy frozen mid-stride, road dust hanging solid in the air, cloth bundles
suspended mid-sway, rickshaw wheels stopped mid-spin. NOTHING moves except the
camera, which drifts through the frozen moment slowly and deliberately, inspecting
details and faces at close range.

KEEP THE ORIGINAL BLACK-AND-WHITE LOOK: monochrome tonality, fine photo grain, hazy
overcast daylight — a living archival photograph. All signage stays EXACTLY as in
the photo and perfectly legible: "121," "ESPLANADE," the Bengali destination
boards, "PUBLIC CARRIER" on the van. The LIFE watermark in the corner remains a
fixed, flat print artifact — not part of the physical scene, unaffected by the
camera's movement. Strictly period-accurate 1960s Calcutta throughout.

CAMERA PATH: the red line is the flight trajectory — enter at the arrow near the
"PUBLIC CARRIER" van's grille (marker 1), end at the arrow exiting the frame toward
the distant row of parked cars, top-left (marker 2).

00–01s: Static frame identical to the input; red markings dissolve. Dust motes hang
motionless in the air — the first hint that time is frozen.

01–04s: The camera dives to marker 1: macro pass over the "PUBLIC CARRIER" van —
grille slats, headlamp glass, windscreen, registration plate. A wisp of road dust
hangs frozen beside the front wheel.

04–08s: The camera rises to the van's DRIVER, arm resting on the window sill, head
frozen mid-turn: extreme close-up — weathered features, a strand of hair caught
mid-flutter, unblinking eyes. Slow half-orbit around his head, focus racking across
his face.

08–12s: Glide up into the cluster of PORTERS carrying loads on their heads — frozen
mid-step, muscles taut, ropes and woven baskets locked in place, a cloth-wrapped
bundle suspended mid-sway. Close orbit around the straining hands and shoulders,
weaving the same S-curve as the drawn path.

12–16s: Sweep right past the CYCLIST walking his bicycle and the parked jeep behind
him: spokes stopped mid-turn, grit kicked off the road hanging in the air. The
camera threads THROUGH the frozen dust cloud, particles parting around the lens in
sharp macro focus.

16–20s: Low pass through the crowd near the tram: a BOY frozen mid-run, one foot
lifted off the ground, shirt caught mid-flap; beside him, men in dhoti and kurta
frozen mid-stride. Slow close-ups on two or three faces — period features, no
morphing.

20–24s: The camera rises along the front of the TRAM: macro on the "121" plate, the
wire-mesh windscreen guard, the "ESPLANADE" board and Bengali signage beneath it,
rivets and chipped paint on the hull.

24–28s: Continue climbing left, sweeping past the distant crowd and shopfronts
fading into haze, toward the far row of PARKED CARS at the top-left of frame — tram
wires overhead frozen in place. The camera settles at marker 2, looking back down
the length of the frozen street.

28–30s: THE PAYOFF — time suddenly UNFREEZES: the tram bell rings, dust scatters,
the porters lurch forward, the boy's foot lands mid-run, and the frame cuts to
black mid-motion.

Physics & consistency: absolute freeze until 28s — not a single object or person
moves; rock-solid geometry on every face and letterform from every angle; no
morphing, no melting features; the camera never clips through objects.

AUDIO: a frozen world — near silence with a low, dreamlike drone; soft airy
whooshes as the camera passes objects; faint reverberant echoes of a street
suspended in time. At 28s sound SNAPS on with the unfreeze: tram bell, engine
clatter, crowd noise, full street ambience for the final two seconds.
```

## Example 2 — Protest Barricade (Spiral Path, Slow-Motion Suspension, Color)

Source: present-day color photograph of a police barricade standoff at a protest;
red spiral drawn over the scene = the camera's one continuous banking arc. Note:
this prompt tells the model the first frame matches the CLEAN plate (no markings).

```
Image-to-video, 30 seconds, ONE continuous flying camera shot — no cuts, no cross-
fades, no whip-pans, no snapping between positions. Every transition is the camera
physically arcing through space along a single unbroken spiral loop.

FIRST FRAME: opens as an exact match of the clean-plate photograph (no red markings
present) — a present-day color photograph of a police barricade standoff at a
protest. Natural documentary color grade, overcast daylight, handheld
photojournalism grain, present-day, unstylized. Every face, uniform, barricade,
banner, and the Indian tricolor flags stay exactly as photographed and legible at
every angle.

CONCEPT — SLOW-MOTION SUSPENSION: time drops into extreme slow motion, not a hard
freeze — chests rise and fall, eyes blink, smoke curls, flag fabric ripples in slow
continuous motion, like 1000fps stretched across 30 seconds. Only the camera moves
at normal speed.

CAMERA PATH — ONE UNBROKEN SPIRAL LOOP: the camera traces a single rising-and-
falling spiral over the standoff, never reversing hard or snapping — one drone
banking through a continuous loop, not a series of stops. The loop: low over the
right-side protestors, lateral left along the barricade to the police, continuing
low behind the police line to the van, the SAME arc curving upward into a wide
elevated pass over the whole barricade, the arc cresting and continuing its natural
downward curve into the tear-gas haze on the right, the arc bottoming out and
rising one final time into the tent. The camera is always mid-arc, already banking
into the next curve before the last one finishes.

00–01s: Static beat matching the plate; a wisp of tear-gas haze drifts almost
imperceptibly.

01–06s: Low arc toward the RIGHT side of the barricade — close orbit on two or
three protestors' faces and the man being hoisted over the rail; eyes straining, a
shout held mid-breath, fingers tightening on the rail in a slow continuous squeeze,
hair lifting in the breeze. The orbit is already curving left as it completes.

06–10s: The same low arc carries the camera left along the top of the crowd into a
close orbit on the POLICE LINE — jaw clenched, eyes narrowed under the beret brim,
a bead of sweat sliding down a temple, a baton grip tightening. The arc begins
lifting as it completes, curving back behind the line.

10–14s: Still lifting, the arc carries the camera BEHIND the police line to the van
— two officers slow-motion guiding a detained man in, his torso twisting gradually,
a knee lifting into the van bed, fingers closing degree by degree on his arm. The
arc keeps climbing past this beat, already opening into a wider view.

14–18s: The arc crests into a wide elevated pass over the full barricade line —
rows of officers, the fence bowing under the crowd's weight, photographers mid-
shutter, placards at odd angles, everything carrying the same faint breathing
motion. This is the highest point of the loop — already curving down and right as
it crests.

18–22s: The arc's natural downward curve carries the camera into the tear-gas smoke
— a canister mid-burst, orange-white tendrils unfurling; a hand rising to shield
streaming eyes, a body caught mid-cough, tears sliding down a cheek. The camera
threads through the drifting smoke, and the smoke's upward draft feeds directly
into the arc's next rise.

22–29s: The arc rises one final time, climbing clear of the haze and curving in
through the open side of the tent. Inside: a tight cluster gathered around one man
holding a megaphone to his mouth, mid-call, neck veins faintly tensed, chest drawn
in a slow inhale; hands raised around him, an Indian tricolor flag held aloft,
faces turned toward him. The arc settles into a slow closing orbit around the
group.

29–30s: THE PAYOFF — slow motion snaps to real time: the megaphone call rings out,
the group surges inward and circles together shoulder to shoulder, the flag punches
upward, a roar goes up — cut to black mid-motion, holding on the tent.

Physics & consistency: one unbroken spiral arc start to finish, the camera always
banking into its next curve rather than stopping or reversing; continuous micro-
motion throughout, no large movement until 29s; rock-solid geometry on every face,
uniform, and flag from every angle; no morphing; the camera never clips through
people, barricades, the van, or the tent fabric; no red lines or drawn markings
anywhere.

AUDIO: a slowed, muffled world — a deep heartbeat-like drone timed to the
breathing, distant shouts and sirens stretched into a low moan, a soft rhythmic
hiss of drifting tear gas, wind swelling briefly during the wide sweep. Entering
the tent the drone thins onto the speaker's held breath. At 29s sound SNAPS to full
speed: the megaphone call, the crowd's roar, flag-fabric snap, full ambience for
the final frame.
```

## Example 3 — Benares Ghats, Varanasi (Frozen Time + Micro-Motion, Color)

Source: present-day color photograph of moored boats at the Varanasi ghats; red
line from the red boat's bow (marker 1) sweeping across the scene and ending low
over the water on the right (marker 2).

```
Image-to-video, 30 seconds, ONE continuous flying drone shot, no cuts, no hard
stops. The camera is a single handheld/drone rig that never stops moving and never
jump-cuts between subjects — every close-up is reached by continuing the same
unbroken glide, arcing and easing smoothly in and out of each stop along the path,
exactly like the one flowing red line in the reference image.

FIRST FRAME: the video starts as an EXACT copy of the attached image — a
contemporary color photograph of moored boats at the ghats of Varanasi on the
Ganges, including the red drawn line with its arrow-marked start at the red boat's
bow, far left, and its arrow-marked end low over the water on the right. Within the
first half second the red markings dissolve completely and must NEVER reappear.

CONCEPT — FROZEN TIME: as the markings vanish, the photograph gains full three-
dimensional depth. Time is not perfectly paused — it crawls at a tiny fraction of
real speed. Every boat, pilgrim, bird and ripple of water is suspended in near-
total stillness, but the ONE subject the camera is studying at any given moment
carries a subtle, extreme-slow-motion micro-movement — a fraction of a blink, a
bead of water sliding down a lota, a feather flexing — before settling back into
stillness as the camera moves on to the next subject. Everything else in frame
stays completely motionless throughout. NOTHING moves at normal speed except the
camera, which drifts through the frozen moment slowly and deliberately.

KEEP THE ORIGINAL COLOR LOOK: natural documentary color photography, hazy overcast
daylight, soft flat light with no hard shadows, the olive-green murky tone of the
Ganges water, faint humidity haze over the river. All detail stays EXACTLY as in
the photo and perfectly legible: the red, white and yellow boat hulls, the tire
fenders lashed along the gunwales, the Hindi lettering ("अल्ट्राटेक सीमेंट") painted on
the yellow boat's hull, the two twin ornamental towers with their painted panels
and cloth canopies, the crowd gathered on the ghat steps behind them, and the ochre
riverside havelis in the background. Strictly consistent with the source photograph
throughout — no invented architecture, no color shift.

CAMERA PATH: the red line is the flight trajectory — enter at the arrow by the red
boat's bow, far left (marker 1), sweep right low across the moored boats toward the
ghat steps, circle in close on an OLD MAN frozen mid-prayer among the crowd, move
to a shaded spot near the twin towers where a GURUJI sits frozen in meditation,
glide to the water's edge where a WOMAN is frozen mid-pour from a copper lota,
climb high above the ghat to a BIRD frozen mid-flight against the sky, then swoop
back down across the towers and boats and exit low over the river's surface on the
right, where a FISH is frozen mid-leap and the line ends in an arrow (marker 2).

00–01s: The frame holds identical to the input for a beat as the red markings
dissolve — but the camera is already drifting forward, never fully static; the
river's surface stays almost perfectly still, a faint haze hanging motionless in
the humid air, the first hint that time is barely alive.

01–05s: Without any cut, the drift becomes a full glide: the camera lifts off from
marker 1 at the RED BOAT'S BOW and sweeps low across the moored hulls toward the
ghat steps in one unbroken forward motion — the boats and their mooring ropes
completely still, tire fenders barely grazed by the frozen ripple of the water, the
camera never slowing to a stop, only curving as it goes.

05–09s: Still gliding forward with no break in motion, the camera arcs up off the
water and curves in low over the crowd on the steps, easing into a slow orbit
around an OLD MAN frozen mid-prayer: palms pressed together, eyes closed, lips just
parted in a chant. As the orbit tightens, his eyelid lowers a fraction in an
achingly slow blink, a strand of his shawl trembles by a hair, then the camera
continues its arc onward without pausing, racking focus across his face as it goes.

09–13s: The same unbroken arc carries the camera onward to a shaded spot near the
twin ornamental towers, curving down toward a GURUJI seated cross-legged, frozen
deep in meditation, eyes closed, hands resting palms-up on his knees. Mid-orbit, a
single prayer bead on his mala slides a whisper further between his fingers, then
the camera keeps drifting, tilting up briefly across the painted panels of the
towers behind him before continuing on.

13–17s: Continuing the same flight with no cut, the camera descends toward the
water's edge, curving in on a WOMAN frozen mid-pour from a copper lota, the stream
of water suspended in a glittering arc toward the river. As the camera glides past
her, a single droplet detaches from the stream and falls a fraction further before
the shot carries on, rising away from her without ever stopping.

17–21s: The glide becomes a long rising arc: the camera climbs high above the ghat
in one continuous ascent, curving past a BIRD frozen mid-flight against the
overcast sky, wings spread wide over the towers and rooftops. At the peak of the
climb, one wingtip flexes a hair further, a feather ripples almost imperceptibly,
then the camera banks and begins its descent without ever pausing at the top.

21–25s: Still in the same unbroken arc, the camera swoops back down in a long
descending curve, passing low over the towers and the line of boats, gathering
speed toward the river's surface on the right — one continuous fall, no stop, no
cut.

25–28s: The descent flattens into a low skimming glide as the camera nears marker
2: a FISH is frozen mid-leap from the surface, its body arced in the air, droplets
suspended around it like a glittering crown, light caught on its wet scales. As the
camera slides past at water level, its tail flicks a fraction, one droplet detaches
and hangs a whisper lower, the camera still moving, now hovering just above the
surface without ever fully stopping.

28–30s: THE PAYOFF — time suddenly UNFREEZES exactly where the camera has come to
rest: the fish completes its leap and splashes back into the Ganges, the woman's
water lands and ripples outward, the bird's wingbeat resumes overhead, the guruji's
mala bead settles, temple bells and river sounds flood in, and the frame cuts to
black mid-splash.

Physics & consistency: the camera never stops, never cuts, and never jump-cuts
between subjects — it is one continuous drone take from marker 1 to marker 2,
always in motion, easing smoothly into and out of every close-up via the same
unbroken arcs, dips and rises traced by the drawn path, with no static holds and no
perceptible edit points anywhere in the 30 seconds. Near-total stillness in the
SCENE until 28s, with only the camera's current focal subject carrying a barely-
perceptible slow-motion micro-movement at a time — every other boat, person, bird
and droplet in frame stays completely still; rock-solid geometry on every face,
boat and structure from every angle; no morphing, no melting features, no warping
of the painted lettering; the camera never touches the water except for the final
low glide.

AUDIO: a frozen world — near silence with a low, dreamlike drone; soft airy
whooshes as the camera passes objects; faint reverberant echoes of a riverside
suspended in time. At 28s sound SNAPS on with the unfreeze: the fish's splash,
water pouring, temple bells, murmured prayers and full ghat ambience for the final
two seconds.
```
